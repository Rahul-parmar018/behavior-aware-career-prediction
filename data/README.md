# Data

Put both files in `data/real/` with these exact names.

## `data/real/ameo_2015.xlsx`: AMEO 2015

Aspiring Minds Employability Outcomes 2015: 3,998 engineering graduates with 10th/12th/college marks, AMCAT
skill scores, and job title, salary and job dates.

- Official release: http://research.aspiringminds.com/resources/ (fill in the request form)
- Public mirror used in the paper: `aspiring_minds_employability_outcomes_2015.xlsx` from
  https://github.com/suy1968/Exploratory-data-analysis (rename it to `ameo_2015.xlsx`)
- Cite: V. Aggarwal, S. Srikant, H. Nisar, "AMEO 2015: A dataset comprising AMCAT test scores, biodata details
  and employment outcomes of job seekers," Proc. 3rd IKDD CODS, 2016.

## `data/real/uci_dropout.csv`: UCI #697

"Predict Students' Dropout and Academic Success", 4,424 students, CC BY 4.0.

- https://archive.ics.uci.edu/dataset/697 (download the zip and rename `data.csv` to `uci_dropout.csv`)
- Cite: V. Realinho, J. Machado, L. Baptista, M. V. Martins, "Predicting student dropout and academic success,"
  Data, 7(11):146, 2022.
